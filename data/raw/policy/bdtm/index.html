The Base District Traffic Models (2024 Update)

The Base District Traffic Models (BDTMs) were developed by the Transport Department for evaluating traffic impact in works projects. BDTMs were initially developed for internal uses.  It is now opened to other government departments/ academic institutions for conducting traffic studies. It is also available for sale to private developers and/or their traffic and transport consultants for accessing traffic impact and reviewing road improvement proposals.

The HKSAR is divided into 10 BDTMs as shown in the following:

1. Island North (West of Victoria Park)
2. Island East (East of Victoria Park)
3. Island South (South of Pokfield Road, Wong Nai Chung Gap and Tai Tam Gap)
4. Kowloon West (West of Victory Avenue)
5. Kowloon East (East of Victory Avenue)
6. Tuen Mun and Yuen Long
7. Kwai Tsing and Tsuen Wan
8. Lantau Island
9. Shatin & North District
10. Sai Kung and Tseung Kwan O
